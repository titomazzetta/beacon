      Tools

      Everything here works offline, even with the AI turned off.

      Saved

      Plans are saved here automatically, and you can save any answer. They open with the AI off and download as PDFs to share. Only you see them until you share them. These were shared by people on this Beacon.

      What's installed

      Install a level of the library. Each level includes everything below it. Downloads need internet once; everything works offline after.

      Brain

      The AI models on this drive. Beacon uses the strongest one the computer it's plugged into can run, so a bigger Mac gets a smarter brain automatically.

      Maps

      Place names let you search for towns. The world map shows countries, roads and cities; a detailed region map adds every street and trail around a place.

      Topics

      Pick individual manuals, guides and encyclopedias. Items marked "link" open the publisher's site instead of downloading.
