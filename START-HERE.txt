=====================================================================
                           B E A C O N
          Your offline survival guide, library and assistant.
               Works with no internet, no cell service.
=====================================================================

WHAT IT IS
  Beacon is a USB drive with a private AI assistant and a library of
  survival manuals, medical guides, maps and books. You type a
  question in plain English; it answers from the library and shows you
  exactly which page or article it used. Nothing is sent anywhere.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
OPEN IT
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  MAC
    1. Plug in the drive. Open it in Finder, then open the Beacon folder.
    2. FIRST TIME ONLY: right-click (or Control-click) "Beacon.command"
       and choose Open, then Open again. macOS asks once.
    3. After that, just double-click Beacon.command.

  WINDOWS PC
    1. Plug in the drive. Open it in File Explorer, then the Beacon folder.
    2. Double-click "Beacon.bat".
    3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info",
       then "Run anyway".

  A black window opens (leave it open; closing it turns Beacon off)
  and your web browser shows Beacon. No internet is needed.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FIRST SCREEN: COMPUTER CHECK
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Beacon looks at the computer it's plugged into (memory, graphics,
  battery, free space) and picks the strongest AI "brain" that
  computer can run. Click Start.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT EACH TAB DOES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  EMERGENCY (red button)  Instant first-aid steps. No AI, no waiting.
  ASK      Type any question. Answers cite their sources; click a
           [number] to read the original page.
  PLAN     Pick a plan (evacuation, winter storm, tornado, leaving the
           country, food, garden...). Fill in what you know. Beacon
           writes a step-by-step plan you can print.
  MAP      Type where you are (or coordinates) and where you need to
           go. See the map, distance, compass direction and travel time.
  LIBRARY  See what's installed. With internet, download more:
           manuals, Wikipedia, maps, books, and bigger AI brains.
  CHECK    Proves the AI can find every manual and library on the drive.
  MY INFO  Your household: people, medical needs, contacts and meeting
           places. Plans use it automatically. Prints in the binder.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
SAVE AS PDF / PRINT
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Click "Save as PDF" on any answer, plan or map.
    Mac:     in the print window, click "PDF" (bottom left) > Save as PDF.
    Windows: choose "Microsoft Print to PDF" as the printer.
  PRINT THE BINDER: Library tab > "Print the binder" prints every quick
  card, the cliff notes and your household info. Keep a paper copy
  with your emergency supplies in case all power is gone.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
TALK INSTEAD OF TYPING
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Mac:     press the microphone key, or the Fn/Globe key twice, then speak.
  Windows: press Windows key + H, then speak.
  Click "Read aloud" under any answer to hear it.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
IF THE AI WON'T START
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Everything important is also plain text you can open directly:
    Beacon > library > manuals > cards
      BEACON-CLIFFNOTES.txt   <- the most important file. Start here.
      first-aid-essentials.md, water.md, severe-weather.md, ...
  Any computer, phone or tablet can open these.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
ADD YOUR OWN DOCUMENTS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Copy PDFs or text files into  Beacon > library > manuals
  (generator manual, medical records, insurance, house info, maps).
  Then in Beacon: Library tab > "Index manuals folder".
  Scanned PDFs (photos of pages) can be stored but not searched.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
GOOD TO KNOW
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The AI can make mistakes. For medical, electrical, gas, weapons or
    wild food questions, click the source and read it yourself.
    Call 911 / 112 whenever you can.
  - On battery power the AI drains the laptop quickly. Use the quick
    cards and the Emergency button when you need to save power.
  - Laptops don't have GPS. Type your town or coordinates on the Map
    tab (a phone's compass app or a paper map can give coordinates).
  - Your household info is stored on this drive WITHOUT a password.
    Keep the drive somewhere safe.
  - To update the library: plug into any computer with internet, open
    Beacon, go to the Library tab.
  - Works best with 16 GB of memory or more. Older and Intel
    computers use a smaller, slower brain, but everything still works.
