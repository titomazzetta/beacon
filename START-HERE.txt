IN AN EMERGENCY, RIGHT NOW
  1. If you can, call 911 (US and Canada) or 112 (Europe, and most
     mobile phones worldwide). In many US areas you can text 911.
  2. Open Beacon (next section) and click "I need help now" at the
     top. Bleeding, CPR, choking, burns, shock and more, step by
     step. No AI, no waiting.
  3. If Beacon won't start, open this file on any computer, phone or
     tablet:
        Beacon > library > manuals > cards > BEACON-CLIFFNOTES.txt
     The other files in that folder are the quick cards.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
OPEN BEACON
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Plug the drive into a Mac. If it's locked, the Mac asks for the
     password.
  2. Open the drive in Finder, then the Beacon folder.
  3. Double-click Beacon. Your web browser opens it. The first start
     takes 1 to 3 minutes while the AI loads from the drive.
  4. Ready when the status button at the top shows a green dot and
     the AI's name (like qwen3:14b).

  Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or newer. Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and later)
  are fast. Intel Macs work, but answers come slowly.

  The Mac says Beacon "can't be opened"? Control-click Beacon,
  choose Open, then Open again. It only asks once.
  Nothing happens? Double-click "Beacon (with Terminal)" instead.
  It shows what's going on.

BEFORE YOU UNPLUG THE DRIVE
  Click the status button at the top, then Quit. Then eject the
  drive (Control-click it in Finder > Eject). Pulling it out while
  Beacon runs can damage the library.

WHAT'S ON THE SCREEN
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  I NEED HELP NOW  Emergency cards that open instantly. Some have
                   tools inside: a CPR beat, a tourniquet timer.
  HOME     Ask anything in plain English, by typing or talking.
           Answers come from the library on this drive and show
           their sources: click a [number] to read the original.
           Below: Your next steps, and updates when they're on.
  PLAN     Pick a plan: evacuation, shelter in place, winter storm,
           hurricane, backup power, homestead, stranded on an
           island, neighborhood network, leaving the country,
           survive and evade, and more. Choose when it's for:
           preparing ahead, happening now, or recovering after.
           Beacon writes a step-by-step plan for your household
           that you can save, print and share.
  MAP      Where you are and where you're going: distance, compass
           direction, travel time, nearest towns. Works offline.
  BOARD    Check-ins (safe or need help), needs, offers and reports
           from everyone on this Beacon, from mesh radios, and from
           other Beacons linked to this one.
  TOOLS    CPR beat, tourniquet timer, rehydration drink, water
           treatment with bleach, water to store, power budget,
           supplies, Morse code, radio channels and a MAYDAY call,
           flashlight and SOS, heat and cold risk, lightning
           distance, sun and moon times, translate, unit
           conversions.
  LIBRARY  Saved plans and answers, what's installed, the health
           check, your own documents, the reading shelf, paper
           backups, and gear worth having. With internet: download
           more manuals, Wikipedia, maps and bigger AI brains, or
           run the builder again; it only adds what's missing.

  The AI can be wrong. For medical, electrical, gas, weapons or
  wild-food decisions, read the source it cites, and get trained
  help whenever you can.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
YOUR NEXT STEPS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Tell Beacon when this is for, what you're facing, who's with you,
  where you are and what you have. "Almost nothing yet" is a fine
  answer. Beacon then shows:
    - what to do first, right now
    - what each thing you have is good for
    - what's missing, most important first, and how to make do
      until you have it
    - the plans, quick cards and manuals that fit
  Every step has a button that opens the card, plan or tool for it.
  It works with the AI off. Change your answers any time from Home.
  The AI also uses them, so answers fit your people and your gear.

  On this Mac, your answers are saved on the drive and phones can't
  see them. On a phone, they stay on that phone.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
SHARE WITH PHONES AND TABLETS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Anyone on the same Wi-Fi can use Beacon in their phone's web
  browser, powered by this Mac. Nothing to install on the phone.
  1. Click Share at the top, then Start sharing. If the Mac asks
     whether Python may accept incoming connections, click Allow.
  2. On the phone, point the camera at the QR code. Or open
     beacon.local (or the number address shown) and type the
     4-digit code.
  3. Keep the Mac plugged in with the lid open. Beacon stops it from
     going to sleep while sharing.

  No internet needed. A home Wi-Fi router keeps working during an
  outage as long as it has power (a power station or a UPS keeps it
  on). A small travel router, or a phone's hotspot, works too.

  Guests can ask, plan, read, check in and save files to their
  phone. They can't change settings, download anything new, see your
  household info or setup answers, or quit Beacon.

  WHO'S CONNECTED: Share > Connections lists every device signed in,
  every wrong code and anything Beacon blocked, and the Mac pops up
  an alert when someone fails or gets blocked. "Sign everyone out"
  changes the code. "Stop sharing" disconnects everyone.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
SAVE, PRINT AND KEEP
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Every answer and plan has Save PDF. Your next steps too.
  - Maps have Save image and Print.
  - Plans made on this Mac are kept on the drive automatically;
    answers when you click Save to Beacon. Find them in Library >
    Saved and on the Plan tab. They open even with the AI off.
  - "Share with everyone" puts a saved plan on every phone connected
    to this Beacon.
  - On a phone: Library > Paper backup > All quick cards saves every
    card to the phone as plain text.
  - Library > Paper backup > The binder: every quick card, the cliff
    notes and your household info, ready to print. Keep a paper copy
    with your supplies in case all power is gone.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
THE BOARD, RADIOS AND OTHER BEACONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Board > "We're safe" or "We need help" puts your household on the
  roster everyone sees. Post needs, offers and reports. This Mac can
  pin and remove posts.

  MESH RADIOS (optional). Meshtastic radios send short texts radio
  to radio, with no cell service or internet: a few blocks in a
  dense town, many miles with a clear view. Plug one into this Mac
  with a DATA USB cable and Beacon connects to it by itself. Messages
  from radios appear on the Board, and anyone with a radio can check
  in by sending a text like:
        SAFE The Garcias
        HELP Unit 4B: out of insulin
  A radio with GPS also tells the Map where this Mac is.
  Setup, private channels and range tips: the Mesh radio card.

  OTHER RADIOS. Tools > Radio channels: which walkie-talkie, CB,
  marine and ham channels people listen to, the NOAA weather
  channels, and a MAYDAY call written out from your details. A car
  radio picks up AM and FM emergency broadcasts.

  LINK ANOTHER BEACON. Two Beacons on the same network (a house and
  a shelter, say) can share one Board. On the main Beacon, turn on
  Share. On the other: Board > Linked Beacons, type the main
  Beacon's address and code, and click Link. Check-ins and posts
  then reach everyone on both. Only the Board is shared, and the
  main Beacon can sign the link out under Share > Connections.

  Walkie-talkies, the check-in schedule and roles: the Community
  card. Ask Beacon to make a "Neighborhood network" plan.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
UPDATES WHEN ONLINE  (optional, off until you turn it on)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  When this Mac has internet, Beacon can check official weather
  alerts for your area (US), disaster and earthquake alerts, travel
  advisories and headlines, and keep them on the drive for reading
  offline, on phones too. Turn it on in the setup questions, or
  Home > Updates. Warnings for your area show at the top of Home.
  Nothing about you or your questions is sent. The weather service
  gets your town's rough location, to about a kilometer.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
LOCK THE DRIVE WITH A PASSWORD  (once)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Your household info, setup answers, plans and notes live on this
  drive. Locked, a lost drive is just a lost drive.
  - In Finder, Control-click the drive and choose Encrypt. Pick a
    password you won't forget. Keep it somewhere separate from the
    drive. Encryption finishes in the background.
  - No Encrypt option? The drive is exFAT, which can't be encrypted.
    Copy off anything you added, erase the drive in Disk Utility as
    "APFS (Encrypted)", and build it again.
  - Only Macs can open an encrypted drive, and a forgotten password
    can't be recovered.
  The health check shows whether the drive is locked. To erase your
  household info and setup answers: Plan > Your household > Erase
  all of it.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
POWER
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Answering questions uses much more battery than reading. When
    power is short, click the status button and turn on Low power:
    the AI turns off, and cards, tools, maps and the library keep
    working.
  - Tools > Power budget: how long a power station or solar panels
    will run the things you need.
  - The Off-grid power card covers power stations, solar,
    generator safety and making your own power. The Power outage
    card covers staying safe and warm when the grid goes down.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT SIZE DRIVE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Best: a 1 TB portable SSD (USB-C), like a Samsung T7 Shield,
  SanDisk Extreme or Crucial X9/X10. It holds everything plus bigger
  AI brains and your own files, and loads the AI several times
  faster than a thumb drive.

    Library tier   Drive     What it adds
    Core           64 GB     the essentials
    Extended       128 GB    repair, off-grid, homesteading, books
    Complete       256 GB    Wikipedia intros, Wikibooks, learning
    Everything     512 GB+   all of Wikipedia (1 TB recommended)

  The Library tab shows exact sizes before you download anything.

ADD YOUR OWN DOCUMENTS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Copy PDFs or text files into  Beacon > library > manuals
  (generator and appliance manuals, medical records, insurance,
  local maps, house notes). Then: Library > Manuals and guides >
  Index new files. Scanned PDFs (photos of pages) are kept but can't

---------------------------------------------------------------------
TALK INSTEAD OF TYPING
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Press the microphone key, or the Fn/Globe key twice, and speak.
  (Turn it on in System Settings > Keyboard > Dictation. On Apple
  Silicon Macs it works without internet.)
  "Read aloud" under any answer reads it out.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Beacon won't open
      Double-click "Beacon (with Terminal)" to see why. Logs are in
      Beacon > data > logs.
  "The AI engine isn't running"
      Click the status button, Quit, and open Beacon again.
  "The AI model isn't on this drive"
      Library > Brain. Downloading needs internet once.
  Answers are very slow
      Plug in the charger. Older and Intel Macs are slower: in
      Library > Brain, choose a lighter brain.
  A phone can't open beacon.local
      Use the number address shown under Share. The phone must be on
      the same Wi-Fi, and not a guest network (those often keep
      devices apart).
  Alerts about wrong codes you didn't expect
      Share > Connections shows which device. Sign everyone out, or
      Stop sharing.
  The mesh radio isn't found
      Use a data USB cable (some only charge) and another port. Set
      the radio's region in the Meshtastic app first.
  A linked Beacon says "Signed out" or "Can't reach it"
      Turn on Share on the main Beacon and link again with its
      current code. Both must be on the same network.
  Anything else
      Ask Beacon. It has the cards, the manuals and your documents.
