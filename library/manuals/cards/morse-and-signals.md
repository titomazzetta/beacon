# Morse Code & Rescue Signals Card

## SOS
**· · · — — — · · ·**  (3 short, 3 long, 3 short), sent as one unbroken signal, then pause and repeat.
Works with a flashlight, car headlights, a whistle, a car horn, a mirror flash, or by tapping on pipes or walls.

## Timing
- Dot = 1 unit. Dash = 3 units.
- Gap inside a letter = 1 unit. Gap between letters = 3 units. Gap between words = 7 units.

## Useful words
- **SOS** = help · **CQ** = calling anyone · **K** = over, your turn · **AR** = end of message · **R** = received
- **QTH** = my location is… · **QRZ** = who is calling me?

## Rescue signals (anywhere in the world)
- **Three of anything = distress**: 3 whistle blasts, 3 fires in a triangle, 3 gunshots, 3 flashes. Repeat every few minutes.
- **Ground-to-air symbols** (make them big, at least 10 feet / 3 m tall, high contrast; use rocks, logs, clothing, or stamped snow):
  - **V** = I need help · **X** = I need medical help · **N** = No · **Y** = Yes · **→** = I'm going this way
- **Signal mirror** (or a phone screen, CD, polished metal): hold your other hand out in a V and flash the reflection through it toward the aircraft or ship. Sweep the horizon even if you see nothing. Flashes can be seen many miles away.
- **Smoke**: green leaves or rubber on a fire make thick white or black smoke by day. A bright fire at night.
- **Whistle beats shouting**: it carries farther and doesn't wear out your voice.
- **Body signals to aircraft**: both arms raised in a Y = "Yes / I need help." One arm up, one down in a straight diagonal = "No / I don't need help."

## Radio
See the Emergency Radio card for frequencies. If no one answers by voice, Morse cuts through noise and weak signals far better.
